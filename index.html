About Me

I'm a data/research analyst with 5 years of experience owning projects end-to-end; from project design, to data collection, cleaning, preparation, validation, and analysis, to reporting that decisionmakers actually use to make decisions. I've worked with brands like H-E-B, TelevisaUnivision, and Jersey Mike's. One project (a multi-state 2024 election tracker) even got cited by Politico and NPR.

I have a BS in Computer Science from WGU, and I've been building on that foundation to go deeper on the technical side: more SQL, more Python, and Tableau instead of just Excel and Powerpoint. My personal projects reflect that: a streaming service value analysis, a full breakdown of the history of the Houston Texans, and a machine-learning-heavy project designed to predict whether a certain baseball pitch will be a strike.

I'm currently looking for opportunities where I can grow as an analyst while working on problems that make a real difference for the people who use the results. I am currently located in Houston, TX, but am open to opportunities for relocation.
